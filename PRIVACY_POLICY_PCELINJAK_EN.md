# Privacy Policy – Pčelinjak

**Last updated:** August 9, 2026.

**Other language:** [Srpski](./PRIVACY_POLICY_PCELINJAK.md)

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Pčelinjak** application ("app", "we") collects, uses, and protects your data. By using the app and the related service at [pcelinjak.hexatech.rs](https://pcelinjak.hexatech.rs), you accept the terms described in this policy.

The app is developed by Hexatech. Android package name: `rs.hexatech.pcelinjak`. For privacy questions, contact us using the details in the Contact section.

---

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 Data you provide

- **Account:** email address, name, phone (optional), password (stored in hashed form). Required for registration, account activation, and sign-in.
- **Apiary data:** apiaries, hives, queens, notes, harvests, inspections, work groups, reminders, and related records you enter. Stored locally on the device and, when you sync, on our server linked to your account.
- **Feedback:** if you send feedback from the app, we store the message and email to improve the service.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

- **Technical data:** device type, operating system, app version – to the extent needed to operate the service and fix errors.
- **Device identifier / push token:** to deliver reminders and notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and to keep one active device per account.
- **Server logs:** IP address, request time, and technical error information, without profiling for marketing purposes.

### 2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not sell your data. We do not collect background location, track you for advertising, or share your data with third parties for marketing. The app does not display ads.

---

## 3. Purpose of processing

We use your data only to:

- **Provide the service:** account, local records, and sync of apiary data.
- **Notifications:** local and push reminders related to inspections and apiary work.
- **Security:** protect accounts, activate accounts, and prevent misuse (including one active device per account).
- **Legal obligations:** where required by law.
- **Improve the service:** fix errors and improve the app.

---

## 4. Legal basis for processing

- **Performance of a contract:** processing is necessary to provide the service you accepted.
- **Consent:** where required (e.g. notification permission on the device).
- **Legitimate interest:** for security, abuse prevention, and operating the service.

---

## 5. Data retention

- Server data is stored in Europe (Hetzner, Germany), in accordance with applicable rules.
- We keep data while your account is active.
- Passwords are not stored in readable form; we use industry-standard hashing.
- Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.
- Local data remains on the device until you delete the account or the app.

---

## 6. Sharing with third parties

- **Hosting and infrastructure:** we use trusted providers for servers, databases, and backups. Processing is limited to providing the service on our behalf.
- **Firebase (Google):** for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging). Tokens and technical identifiers are processed under Google/Firebase policies, only to deliver notifications.
- **Legal obligations:** we may be required to disclose data to competent authorities when legally required.

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 7. Your rights

Under applicable law (including GDPR where it applies), you may:

- request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data;
- restrict processing or object to processing;
- lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in the Republic of Serbia: Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection).

**Account deletion:** you can delete your account in the app (Settings), with password confirmation. This removes data on the server and locally on the device.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below.

## 8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

## 9. Children
