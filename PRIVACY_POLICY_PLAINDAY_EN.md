# Privacy policy – Plainday

**Last updated: 15 August 2026. · [Data deletion](https://hexatech.rs/account-deletion/plainday)**

**Plainday** is a Hexatech day-control app. It works locally on your device. No account is required.

## Summary

- Profiles, timers, breaks, reminders and reports are stored on your device.
- We do not run an account and do not send your content to our servers.
- We do not sell data and do not show ads.

## Information stored on your device

Content you create (profiles, schedule, day log, reports) and data needed for local notifications and the widget. This is not uploaded to Hexatech servers.

## Information we do not collect

We do not ask for a name, email or phone for an account. We do not collect background location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

## Internet and third parties

The app is designed to work without sending your content to us. Local reminder notifications stay on the device. Distribution through Google Play is subject to Google’s policies. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics SDKs.

## Children

Plainday is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it in the app or uninstall Plainday (subject to Android backup settings). See [data deletion](https://hexatech.rs/account-deletion/plainday).

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page will change when we do.
