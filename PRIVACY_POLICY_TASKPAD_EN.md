# Privacy policy – Taskpad

**Last updated: 6 July 2026. · [Data deletion](https://hexatech.rs/account-deletion/taskpad)**

Taskpad ("the app") is a simple offline notes and tasks application published by Hexatech ("we", "us", or "our").

## Summary

- Taskpad works offline.
- Your notes and tasks are stored only on your device.
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not require an account.
- We do not collect personal information for our own use.

## Information the app stores on your device

When you use Taskpad, the app stores the content you create locally on your phone or tablet, including:

- note titles and note text
- task titles, completion status, due dates, and priority levels
- widget preview data needed to show active tasks on your home screen

This information is saved in the app's local storage on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers because we do not operate servers that receive your app content.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, or store the following from you:

- name, email address, or phone number through the app
- location data
- contacts, photos, or files outside the app
- analytics or advertising identifiers for our own tracking
- payment information

## Internet access

Taskpad is designed to function without sending your notes or tasks to us. The app may include technical permissions or libraries used by the operating system or development framework. Even if an internet-related permission is present, the app does not use your personal content for online services operated by us.

## Third-party services

Taskpad does not include third-party advertising and does not use third-party analytics SDKs in the app. Distribution of the app through Google Play is subject to Google's own policies and services.

## Children's privacy

Taskpad is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention and deletion

Your notes and tasks remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall Taskpad. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data from your device, subject to your device and Android backup settings. See [data deletion](https://hexatech.rs/account-deletion/taskpad).

## Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security also depends on your device protections such as screen lock, device encryption, and operating system updates.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are made.
