# Account deletion – Farma

**Last updated: 14 August 2026. · [Privacy policy](https://hexatech.rs/privacy/farma)**

To delete your account and farm data, send a request from the account email to [hexatech.rs@gmail.com](mailto:hexatech.rs@gmail.com).

State that you want the Farma account deleted. Deletion is permanent and includes farms, animals, events and reminders linked to the account. We respond within 30 days.
