# Data deletion – Plainday

**Last updated: 14 August 2026. · [Privacy policy](https://hexatech.rs/privacy/plainday)**

Plainday has no server account. Profiles, timers and reports stay on the phone only.

- Delete content in the app, or
- uninstall Plainday to remove locally stored app data (subject to Android backup settings).
