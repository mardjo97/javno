# Privacy policy – Farma

**Last updated: 15 August 2026. · [Account deletion](https://hexatech.rs/account-deletion/farma)**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Farma** app (“the app”, “we”) collects, uses, and protects your data. By using the app, you accept the terms described in this policy.

The app is developed and operated by the Farma team (Hexatech). For privacy questions, you can contact us using the details at the end of this document.

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 Data you provide

- **Account:** email, name and password (stored in hashed form). These are required to create an account and sign in.
- **Content you enter in the app:** data about farms, groups, animals, events, and reminders. Stored on our servers so you can access it from your device.
- **Feedback:** if you send a suggestion, bug report or complaint, the message is stored on the server to improve the app.

### 2.2 Data we collect automatically

- **Technical data:** device type, operating system, app version, usage time — only as needed to run the service, fix bugs, and improve the app.
- **Device identifiers:** we do not use advertising and do not track you outside the app. Identifiers may be used only for technical purposes (e.g. push notifications, if enabled).

### 2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not sell your data. We do not collect location in the background, track you for advertising, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 3. Purpose of processing

We use your data only to:

- **Provide the service:** sign-in, sync, and storage of your farms, animals, events, and reminders.
- **Email:** account activation and password reset from noreply@hexatech.rs.
- **Security:** protect accounts and prevent abuse.
- **Legal obligations:** where required by law.
- **Improve the service:** anonymized or aggregated statistics to improve the app (without identifying individual users, as permitted by law).

## 4. Legal basis for processing

- **Performance of a contract:** processing is necessary to provide the service you accepted (account and use of the app).
- **Your consent:** where required, we will ask for your consent (e.g. optional notifications or additional features).
- **Legitimate interest:** for security, protection against abuse, and improving service quality to the extent permitted by law.

## 5. Data retention

- Data is stored on servers in Europe (Hetzner, Germany), in accordance with applicable rules.
- We keep data while your account is active and for the period needed to meet legal obligations after account deletion.
- Passwords are not stored in readable form; we use industry-standard hashing and protection. Traffic uses HTTPS.

## 6. Sharing data with third parties

- **Hosting:** Hetzner (servers and database) — processing only on our behalf.
- **Email:** SMTP (Loopia) for activation and password reset.
- **Google Play:** Android distribution is subject to Google’s policies.
- **Legal obligations:** if required by law (e.g. a court order), we may have to disclose data to competent authorities.
- **Sale or transfer:** if the company or its assets are sold, data may be part of that transfer; in that case we will notify you as required by law.

We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 7. Your rights

Under applicable rules (including the GDPR, where applicable), you have the right to:

- **Access:** request confirmation of whether we process your data and a copy of that data.
- **Rectification:** request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure:** request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”), subject to exceptions allowed by law.
- **Restriction of processing:** request that processing be temporarily restricted in certain situations.
- **Portability:** request your data in a structured, commonly used format (where technically feasible).
- **Objection:** object to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Complaint:** lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority (in the Republic of Serbia: the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection).

To exercise these rights, send a request to the contact below. We will respond within the time required by law (typically within 30 days). Account deletion: [instructions](https://hexatech.rs/account-deletion/farma).

## 8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse (encryption, limited access, regular checks).

## 9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 16. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 16 without parental consent, we will take steps to delete that data.

## 10. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. The last updated date will appear at the beginning of this document. Continued use of the app after changes are published is considered acceptance of the new version.
