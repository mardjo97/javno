# Privacy policy – Moja Faktura

**Last updated: 15 August 2026. · [Account deletion](https://hexatech.rs/account-deletion/moja-faktura)**

## 1. Introduction

This policy describes how the **Moja Faktura** web app (“app”, “we”) collects, uses and protects data. The app is developed by Hexatech.

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 Data you provide

- **Account:** email and password (hashed).
- **Your company:** name, address, tax/registration IDs, signature, bank accounts.
- **Customers and invoices:** customer details you enter (e.g. name, address, tax ID, email, phone) and invoice content. This may include personal data of third parties. You are responsible for having a basis to enter it; we process it so you can issue and export invoices.

### 2.2 Data collected automatically

Technical data and server logs (device type, IP, request time) as needed to run the service and fix errors, without marketing profiling.

### 2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not sell data. No ads and no sharing with third parties for marketing.

## 3. Purpose

Sign-in, storing company settings, customers and invoices, PDF export, account security and legal obligations.

## 4. Legal basis

- **Contract performance** with you as the account holder.
- **Legitimate interest:** security and operating the service.
- For customer data you enter, you determine the purpose; we are a processor in that part.

## 5. Retention

- Servers in Europe (Hetzner, Germany).
- Data while the account is active and as required by law after deletion.
- Passwords are not stored in readable form. HTTPS.

## 6. Sharing

- **Hosting:** Hetzner — only on our behalf.
- **Law:** disclosure if required by a competent authority.

We do not sell data for marketing.

## 7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, object to processing, or lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection (Serbia). Email the contact below; we respond within the time required by law (typically within 30 days). Account deletion: [instructions](https://hexatech.rs/account-deletion/moja-faktura).

## 8. Security

Encryption in transit, password hashing and limited server access.

## 9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 16.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date is at the top of this document.
