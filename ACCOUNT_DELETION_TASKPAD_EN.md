# Data deletion – Taskpad

**Last updated: 6 July 2026. · [Privacy policy](https://hexatech.rs/privacy/taskpad)**

Taskpad has no server account. Notes and tasks stay on the device only.

- Delete items in the app, or
- uninstall Taskpad to remove locally stored app data (subject to Android backup settings).
